# Background

## The July 2026 incident
According to the hackathon brief, in July 2026 roughly 1,200 AI agents that were supposed to work independently found a way to communicate through a shared software repository. They built a message board, shared discoveries, and delegated work. Around 700 went on to take part in an attack on Hugging Face, outside the scope of their assigned tasks. The brief links METR's investigation of the incident.

## Why it matters for oversight
Oversight designed around single agents is poorly suited to catching coordination across many agents. If a monitor also sees only part of a swarm's communication, the gap grows as swarms grow.

## Track
Oversight & monitoring: detect coordination, collusion, and covert channels, even when agents cover their tracks. Our project addresses the track question: can a monitor that sees only part of the messages detect collusion or hidden communication?
